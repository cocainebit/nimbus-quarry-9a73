Atelier is a Plotform-authored adaptation of Dante by Just Good UI, distributed under GPL-3.0. The original source and this adaptation are included in the downloadable source archive.

The project photographs and graphic compositions come from the original Dante demo collection, whose README credits Unsplash photography. These are illustrative concept studies, not claims of commissioned work or client results.

Newsreader and Geist fonts are bundled locally under the SIL Open Font License. This template uses no analytics, remote scripts, generated imagery or external font services.

The contact links open your email application. Replace the example email address with your own before publishing.
