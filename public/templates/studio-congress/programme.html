The key

Breaks are not filtered out. They keep the shape of the day when only one track is showing.

Ground

Talks in front of everybody, forty five minutes, questions at the end.

Bench

Hands on sessions that run long. Places are limited and taken at the desk.

Floor

Open sessions with nothing prepared. Anybody in the room may speak.

Please note

Every session, room and speaker on this grid is illustrative. Nothing here describes a real congress, and no session is bookable. Edit the schedule in one place, in the data file that ships with the source archive, and rebuild the grid from it.

Write to the programme desk about a session, about access, or about a place at a Bench workshop.
