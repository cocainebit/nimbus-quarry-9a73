Source and provenance

Built on shared foundations.

Strand is a Plotform-authored adaptation of AstroWind, the MIT-licensed Astro business template, pinned at commit 14e1a691f80548dcc36370847b1a02c0d0b12821. It keeps AstroWind's multi-page information architecture, its centred header with a single action, its alternating text-and-image content rows, its native details-based accordion and its footer structure, rebuilt as portable HTML, CSS and a small script with no build step.

The composition, typography, copy, floor-plan drawings, season switcher and colour system are new work for this edition and are released under the MIT license in the accompanying LICENSE file. The original AstroWind notice is kept in the licenses folder and the pinned upstream source is included in the downloadable archive.

Type

Playfair Display and DM Sans, both under the SIL Open Font License, bundled locally from their Fontsource packages. Their license texts are in the licenses folder. No remote fonts, scripts, analytics or forms are used; enquiries are plain email links to an example address.

The content

The house, its rooms, the menu, the walks and the seasons are illustrative. There is no real Strand at this address, no rates are quoted, and nothing on these pages should be read as a review, a rating or a claim about a real business. Replace the words and pictures with your own before publishing.
