The house

A handful of rooms, a long table, and a door that is usually open.

Strand is a house at the edge of the water, kept the way a house is kept when people live in it: fires lit, bread in the oven, boots by the door. It is not a hotel and does not try to be one.

Rooms are let by the night or the week. Dinner is cooked when asked for. The rest of the day belongs to the tide, the weather, and whatever you brought to read.

The table

Cooked from whatever the morning brought in.

There is one long table and one sitting. What is on it depends on the boats, the garden and the season, so the menu is written the same afternoon and read out rather than printed.

Breakfast is laid out and left for you. Dinner is by arrangement: tell us by lunchtime and we will set a place.
