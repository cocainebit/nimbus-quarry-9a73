The studio

A small practice, drawn by hand.

Lintel is an architecture and interiors studio. We keep the team small so that the people who draw the first plan are the same people who answer the builder's questions on site.

01

How we work

Four steps, in order

Every project follows the same four steps. They are simple to say and hard to keep to, which is why we write them down.

01Walk+

Every project starts on site, at the time of day the rooms will be used most. We photograph the light and listen to how the place is used before we draw a line.

02Draw+

Plans first, by hand, then sections. If a room cannot be explained in a single drawing, it is not finished yet.

03Build+

We stay through construction, answering questions on site and adjusting details as the building shows us what it wants to be.

04Return+

After handover we come back, look at how the rooms are actually used, and write down what we would do differently next time.

What we take on

Three kinds of room

We say no to most things so that we can say yes properly to a few. These are the projects we look for.

  1. iHouses, new and altered
  2. iiSmall cultural rooms: libraries, halls and galleries
  3. iiiPlaces of work: studios, workshops and offices

Sources and credits

Lintel is a Plotform-authored template. It adapts the page structure and section components of AstroWind, released under the MIT license, into a portable HTML and CSS site for an architecture practice. The complete adapted source is in source.zip, with the upstream notice in the licenses folder.

The projects are illustrative concept studies. The plan drawings are original vector artwork made for this template. Contact links open your email application; replace the example address before publishing.
