Project 01, residential

Two Walls House

All projects

A narrow plot between two party walls. Rather than fight the constraint, the house takes the walls as its edges and opens every room onto a court between them.

Type

Concept study

Two long walls do the work. Everything else is light.

The party walls are thickened into the structure of the house, carrying the roof and holding the services, so the rooms between them can be free of columns and cupboards. A court sits at the centre of the plan and every room borrows its daylight from there rather than from the street.

Because the front wall can stay almost blind, the house is quiet on the road and open in the middle. Bedrooms take the far end, where the walls step down into the garden and become the edges of a terrace.

The plan is drawn as one continuous bar, with the court reading as a room that happens to have no roof.

In the detail

Light comes from the court, not the street, so the front can stay almost blind.
