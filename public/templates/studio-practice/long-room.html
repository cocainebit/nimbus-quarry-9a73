Project 03, workplace

Long Room

All projects

A studio and workshop for a small maker, in a corridor building one room wide. The plan keeps the whole floor open and gathers every service into a single thick wall.

One thick wall, one long room, and nothing in between.

The building was a corridor with rooms hung off one side. We took the rooms away and kept the corridor, then made its blind wall thick enough to hold everything a workshop needs: the stores, the kitchen, the washrooms, the extract, the power.

What remains is a single room lit from one side, long enough for benches at one end and a quiet table at the other. The joinery is drawn as a set of deep shelves that stand free of the walls, so the room can be rearranged without touching the building.

The ceiling is lined with a fluted board that softens the sound of the machines and catches the light from the long window in a slow gradient.

In the detail

The thick wall holds the services so the room can hold the work.
