Retention

What makes subscribers stay

A subscription page should promise the things that reduce churn, and only the ones you deliver. Six that matter.

A fast first win

Onboarding that gets the subscriber to the first result in the first session, before the second charge is even close.

A predictable rhythm

New value on a schedule people can anticipate. Irregular delivery reads as neglect, even when the total is the same.

Visible new value

A changelog, a “new this month” email, a badge in the library. Subscribers should never wonder what they paid for.

A pause option

Pausing keeps a subscriber who would otherwise cancel. Offer it before the cancel button, not instead of it.

Transparent billing

A reminder before every renewal, receipts that explain themselves, no price change without notice.

An exit without friction

Cancelling in one click is the strongest reason to sign up in the first place. Make it true and say it.

FAQ

Questions people ask before they subscribe

Billing, cancellation and value questions, answered before checkout. The same answers feed a FAQ rich result in search.

What makes a subscription landing page different from a pricing page?

A pricing page compares plans for people who already decided to buy. A subscription landing page has to sell the recurring relationship first: what arrives every month, why it stays valuable, and how easy it is to leave.

Monthly or yearly first?

Show both, highlight yearly, and state the saving in plain units (“2 months free”), not percentages. Let the monthly price anchor the value before the yearly discount lands.

How prominent should the cancellation policy be?

As prominent as the price. “Cancel anytime, in one click, from your account” next to the plans removes the main fear of subscribing and costs nothing if you mean it.

Should I offer a free trial or a money-back window?

One of the two. A trial suits products people need to use to understand; a refund window suits content or services. Either way, say it once, near the price, and honour it without friction.

What keeps subscribers from churning?

What the retention section below lists: a fast first win, a predictable delivery rhythm, visible new value, a pause option and transparent billing. The landing page should promise only the ones you deliver.
