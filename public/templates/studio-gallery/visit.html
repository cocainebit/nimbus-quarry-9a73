One floor, six rooms, a courtyard in the middle.

Thursday evenings are the quietest hours of the week. The study room stays open late on the first Thursday of the month, when there is usually someone from the programme office there to talk to.

Access and getting here

Anything not covered here can be asked by email before you come.

In the building

Everything is on one floor and the thresholds are level, including the door from the courtyard. The gravel in the courtyard is rolled flat along the path between the gate and the entrance.

There are benches in Room 2, the upper hall and the courtyard. Folding stools are kept by the desk and can be carried into any room.

The sound piece in the upper hall is loudest in the middle of the room and quietest at the courtyard end.

Coming to the door

The gate on Example Lane is the only way in. It is set back from the street between two brick walls, and it is open whenever the rooms are.

There is a tram stop two streets north and a cycle rack inside the gate. There is no car park, and the lane is narrow, so a taxi is best asked to stop at the corner.
