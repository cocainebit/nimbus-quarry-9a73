Ground Plane

Rooms 1 and 2, from 5 December 2026.

Ground Plane takes the floor as the subject. The rooms are emptied of everything above knee height, five plaster planes are set at a shallow angle, and the hatching that usually stays on a drawing is carried onto the boards.

The work is installed over two weeks in November. The rooms stay open while it happens, so the first thing the exhibition shows is itself being put together.
